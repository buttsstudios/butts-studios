Runwae & GLB

Two clothing labels

Runwae Apparel

Started in Fayetteville, Arkansas. Runwae's look comes from its first magazine: streetwear set into gilded Renaissance paintings, under the line "Everybody Influences."

Two lines are ready to come back. Fall '21 includes True Grit, Crafting, Poison and Pave. The Little Things is a one-color line of monograms, line-art faces and "Luv thy neighbor."

GLB

A sister label built from line-art figure drawings. Clean, one-line drawings on tees and hoodies. It shares a hand with The Little Things.

How drops work

Each drop opens for a few weeks. When it closes, we make exactly what was ordered, and every product page shows a ships-by date we commit to. Simple printed pieces may also be available made to order.

The designs are always drawn by hand. Product mockups may be rendered, but the art never is.
