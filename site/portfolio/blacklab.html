About

Blacklab predicts a physical field over a place, such as the strength of a signal, the presence of an object or how either changes over the next few minutes. It fuses a deterministic physics engine, live sensors and fast learned surrogates into one estimate with per-point confidence.

Radio, camera, LiDAR, radar, audio and weather feeds pass through a common adapter format called PixelCloud. RF is described as the first validated physics domain.

A tiling and attribution design lets many nodes predict a shared area together, with results checked through consensus. People can run the desktop client to contribute compute to the network.

The live demo estimates where a tracked object goes next, or how a transmitter covers an area, and is labeled a fast approximate estimate on a fixed scene.
