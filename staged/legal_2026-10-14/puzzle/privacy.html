2. Information we handle

  • Player name. The name you type on the start screen. It is saved in your browser and, when you finish a game, sent with your score and a timestamp to our leaderboard database, where it is shown publicly to other players. Use a nickname, not your real name.
  • Game progress in your browser. Your daily play count, last-played date and premium status are stored in your browser's local storage on your device; they are not sent to us.
  • Purchase details. If you buy the premium unlock, Stripe collects your payment details and shares with us your name, email, the item, amount and payment status.
  • Emails you send us. Your address and whatever you write.
  • Technical request data that every visit creates: IP address, browser and device type, the page requested and the time, processed by our host to deliver the site.

3. How we use it

  • To run the game, show the public leaderboard and enforce daily play limits.
  • To process and support purchases, including restoring a premium unlock.
  • To answer messages you send.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations.

4. Service providers

We rely on the following providers, each of which processes data on our behalf or receives it directly when you use their part of the service:

  • Vercel Inc. (website hosting and delivery). Vercel receives standard request data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and keeps short-lived request logs.
  • Google LLC, Firebase Cloud Firestore (game leaderboard database). Stores the player name, score or time, and timestamp you submit.
  • Stripe, Inc. (payments). When you click a buy button you leave this site for a Stripe Checkout page; Stripe collects your name, email, card and billing details directly under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). We receive your name, email, the item, amount and payment status, never your full card number.
  • Google LLC, Gmail (email). Messages you send us are received and stored in our Google Workspace or Gmail mailbox.

5. Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember your player name and game state under the keys sliding_numbers_player_name, sliding_numbers_daily_tries, sliding_numbers_last_played and sliding_numbers_premium. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings; doing so resets your progress and removes the premium flag on that device (see the Refund Policy for restoring a purchase). Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout pages for fraud prevention.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw consent. California residents have these rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither) and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising their rights. Residents of the EEA and UK have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR and may complain to their local data-protection authority; our legal bases are performance of a contract (purchases, game features), legitimate interests (security, answering enquiries) and consent where we ask for it. Residents of other US states with privacy laws have comparable rights, including the right to appeal a decision we make about a request.

To make a request, email [CONFIRM: privacy inbox; buttsstudios@gmail.com is the only address that receives mail today] from the address connected to your request, or include enough detail (for example a Stripe receipt or the leaderboard name you used) for us to find the information. We respond within 45 days (one month under the GDPR) and may need to verify your identity. You may use an authorised agent.

This site runs no analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking scripts, so there is no tracking for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to switch off. If we ever add such tools, we will honour Global Privacy Control as an opt-out and update this policy first.
