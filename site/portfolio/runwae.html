About

Runwae is a platform for agreements between brands and influencers. Brands post marketing opportunities with a set budget, and influencers find opportunities from brands in their industry and send proposals.

Each gig follows one agreement process: the influencer submits a proposal, the brand accepts it, the influencer posts the agreed content, the brand verifies the post, and the influencer is paid.

Agreements, brand-to-creator messaging and payments sit in one place, with a profile that holds all of a creator's gigs. The Runwae blog carries guides on pricing and finding influencers alongside interviews with creators.

Butts Studios designed the landing site.
