Summary

Everything sold on GLB is a digital product. Nothing physical ships, so there are no returns of goods; this page explains refunds instead. Payment is processed by Stripe, and approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days of approval.

When you can get a refund

  • Within 14 days of purchase, for any reason: full refund on request. Your premium status is removed when the refund is issued.
  • Lost your unlock after clearing browser data or changing device: we restore it rather than refund, on request with your receipt.
  • Charged twice, or charged for something that never unlocked or arrived: full refund, at any time, once confirmed.
  • We stop offering a paid feature you bought: we will restore access another way or refund you.
  • Where local consumer law gives you stronger rights (for example the EU/UK withdrawal right for digital content), those rights apply. For instant digital unlocks, you agree at checkout that delivery starts immediately, which ends the withdrawal period once the content is unlocked.

How to ask

Email buttsstudios@gmail.com with your Stripe receipt (or the email address you paid with) and a sentence on what went wrong. We aim to reply within 3 business days. Please contact us before opening a card dispute; we resolve most problems faster directly.
