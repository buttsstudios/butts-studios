About

Chrona builds dynamic RF and SAR scenarios that run in realtime and in full 3D. Users compose terrain, platforms and emitters, then watch propagation, returns and geometry change as the scenario plays out.

Scenarios can be wargamed with AI in the loop or run in reverse: start from the outcome you want and let Chrona optimize the scenarios that reach it. Propagation is terrain-based, using ITU-R P.2001 with multipath and atmospheric effects.

Chrona also pairs emulated scenarios with real RF receivers for hardware-in-the-loop testing of localization algorithms in repeatable conditions. Its live demo predicts a received-power coverage field from a trained model, offered as a preview before a full electromagnetic solve.
