About

Fasci is a civic engagement platform for organizing campaigns, events, protests and boycotts. Organizers set a goal, milestones and verification criteria, then recruit volunteers and coordinate action across their network.

Participation is verified through GPS presence, timestamped media, witness attestation and transaction receipts, and each action is recorded with a signed participation certificate. Threaded deliberation and signed records build an audit trail that stakeholders can review.

Coalitions can run multi-organization campaigns with shared dashboards, and the Campaign Studio scopes a campaign to a region drawn on a map. Civic organizations can also run member elections with verifiable voting.
