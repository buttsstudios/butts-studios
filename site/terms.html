What this site sells

Working versions

Many titles are sold while they are still being made: a draft manuscript, a draft script, working cuts or working files. Each product page names the stage before you buy (Development, Pre-production, Production or Released), and the stage and version are printed on the file. A working version can change, be cut or be rewritten before release.

Updates. When you buy a working version you get that version, plus every later draft of that same title until its first release, at no extra charge. Buy the v0.1 draft of a book and each later draft of that book is yours too. We will email you at the address used at checkout when an update you are entitled to is ready.

If a title is cancelled before release, you keep what you bought.

Prices are in US dollars and shown before you pay. Sales tax or VAT is added where the law requires it. Payment is taken by our payment processor, Stripe, when you order. Your order is accepted when we email the confirmation.

Delivery

Downloads are delivered by a link on the confirmation page and by email. If a link fails, write to us and we will send a new one.

Supporter memberships and auto-renewal

A membership renews automatically every month at the price shown when you join, charged to the same payment method, until you cancel. We show the price and renewal terms before you pay and email a receipt each time. You can cancel any time by writing to hello@buttsstudios.com; cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month, and you keep access until then. If the price changes, we email you at least 30 days before it applies.
