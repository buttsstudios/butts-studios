The Channels

Episode drafts

Five channels, a sixth coming

A small network of channels, each with one promise and a few repeatable series. Every episode is built from a real source: a dataset, a public-domain text or a primary record. We show the source on screen.

Cool Facts

One surprising, verifiable fact per episode, explained until it stops being surprising. Each one starts from a single number and the table it came from.

Voices & Verses

Great passages, read well, with the context that makes them land. Public-domain texts only, read in the channel's own voice.

WikiSummarize

What the world looked up this week, and why. The most-read Wikipedia pages, explained in our own words and credited to the articles they come from.

HowTube

How-tos that are better shown than read. We pick the techniques where text falls short, then demonstrate them and explain why they work.

Currency Events

The week's international business and money stories, each explained with the one chart that matters. Educational, never investment advice.

EvolvePath · Coming next

Growing up, honestly: adolescence to adulthood, told from a real life. Read in the author's voice, and labeled as synthetic wherever we use a voice model.
