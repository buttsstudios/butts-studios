About

Prophet is a developer engagement platform for technical communities on GitHub, YouTube, Reddit, Mastodon, Twitter and Discord. Teams plan multi-platform campaigns and scheduled posts from one dashboard.

Content generation uses defined personas with consistent voices for tutorials, release notes and community updates, and drafts pass through a human review workflow. Sentiment analysis follows community mood across platforms and surfaces emerging concerns.

Prophet forecasts engagement for each campaign and scores its own predictions against actual results, so teams can see how closely the model tracks.
