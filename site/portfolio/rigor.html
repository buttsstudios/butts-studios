About

Rigor is a collaborative platform for structured argumentation. Groups map claims, evidence and rebuttals as interactive 3D graphs and build them together in real time with live presence.

Built-in AI tools check facts, score source credibility and flag logical fallacies. Typing a topic generates a starter map with a central claim, premises, evidence, a counter-argument and a conclusion, which the group then examines and extends.

Rigor is aimed at policy and advocacy teams, researchers, public institutions and educators, and finished maps can be published for anyone to explore.
