About

Boltzmann is a collaborative multiphysics simulation platform covering electromagnetic, thermal, mechanical and semiconductor physics in one place. Simulations run in the cloud from the browser, with real-time 3D visualization.

Alongside forward simulation, Boltzmann offers inverse device design: describe target specifications and it generates a device architecture with regions, contacts, materials and boundary conditions, ready to simulate.

The Simulation Studio shows convergence, fields and solver status while a run is in progress, and a browser-based IDE edits device configurations with side-by-side 3D viewers. Teams can publish interactive results and track how they are used.

Trained neural surrogates give fast estimates of device fields. The live demo generates a silicon PN diode and runs those surrogates over it, labeled on the page as an approximation of a full physics solve.
