Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy covers buttsstudios.com, run by Butts Studios ("we"). Our apparel shop, store.buttsstudios.com, has its own privacy policy.

What we collect today

Very little. This site has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising or tracking pixels. It stores one setting in your browser (localStorage, key bs-theme) to remember light or dark mode; it never leaves your device. The site sets no cookies.

Our host, Vercel, receives your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, as every web server does, and keeps short-lived logs for security. Your browser loads our typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you.

When checkout opens

Buying a download or a membership will be handled by Stripe, which collects your name, email, billing details and payment card. We receive your name, email, country or billing address for tax, what you bought and the payment reference. We never see your full card number. We use it to deliver your files, send receipts and update emails for titles you bought, run memberships, handle refunds, keep tax records and prevent fraud. We update this page before checkout opens if anything here changes.

Sharing

Only with the providers named above, each limited to its part of the job, and when the law requires it, to protect our rights, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

How long we keep it

Order and tax records: as long as tax law requires. Correspondence: as long as needed to help you. Server logs: kept briefly by our host.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or for a copy of it. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws, and of the UK and EU, have these rights by law; we extend them to everyone. We will confirm the request is yours, answer within 30 days, and never treat you differently for asking. Some order records must be kept for tax law. UK and EU visitors may also complain to their data protection authority. Write to hello@buttsstudios.com.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do no cross-site tracking and never sell or share personal information for advertising, so these signals change nothing here: you are already opted out.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Purchases require you to be 18 or to have a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has sent us information, write to us and we will delete it.

Where it is processed

We and our providers are in the United States, and your information is processed there.

Changes

We update the date above when this policy changes, and say so on the site for significant changes.

Contact

Butts Studios. Email hello@buttsstudios.com.
