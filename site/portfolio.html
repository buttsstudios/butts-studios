01

Huntington Applied

Identity · Website · Project-site system

An applied research company that builds simulation and infrastructure tools. The identity pairs a terracotta mark with warm charcoal and a topographic line field, and the same landing system carries across every project site below.

Palette
  • #A85B43
  • #CC7351
  • #222324
  • #F1EFE8
System
Eyebrow pill, two-tone headline, paired calls to action, one accent colour per project.

Primary mark on charcoal

02

Runwae Apparel

Identity · Print · Product imagery

Streetwear treated as a work of art. The dripping-R seal and the "Everyone Influences" line came first, on business cards and the 2020 magazine that hung the pieces inside gilt-framed paintings. The 2026 product frames carry that idea onto the shop.

Palette
  • Coral
  • Charcoal
  • Gilt
Marks
Dripping-R seal, the full "Runwae Apparel" name, and a hand-drawn monogram kept for The Little Things line.
03

Daily AI Toll

Identity · Data site

A daily record of job cuts attributed to AI. The mark is three falling bars; the site runs on one red, paper and ink, with monospace numerals so the count reads like a ledger.

Palette
  • #A32D2D
  • #EA605F
  • #F5F3EE
  • #1A1916
Type
Monospace for numbers and labels; plain sans for headlines.

Wordmark on ink

04

Latarence

Identity · Personal site

A one-page personal site built around a hand-drawn L² mark and a scribbled pen portrait. Light mode shows the portrait on paper; dark mode swaps in the mark, drawn in chalk on ink. The name is set in handwriting, with a quiet monospace footer.

Palette
  • #F5F3EE
  • #EDE8DF
  • #1A1916
  • #C8B87A
Type
Gloria Hallelujah for the name; Geist Mono for details; Spectral for reading.
