Latarence

Identity · Personal site

A one-page personal site built around a hand-drawn L² mark and a scribbled pen portrait. Light mode shows the portrait on paper; dark mode swaps in the mark, drawn in chalk on ink. The name is set in handwriting, with a quiet monospace footer.

Palette
  • #F5F3EE
  • #EDE8DF
  • #1A1916
  • #C8B87A
Type
Gloria Hallelujah for the name; Geist Mono for details; Spectral for reading.

05

Ridgefield

Identity · Website

An engineering firm for systems that have to perform in the field: hardware, software, simulation and AI. The identity is a solid square mark and a monospace voice, with ink and bone set off by an olive signal green and an ember orange, faint terrain contours behind the type, and live demos you can steer in the browser.

  • #F0EEE8

06

The Signals Institute

Identity · Website

An institute in West Palm Beach increasing access to technology careers in underserved communities. The identity pairs a bee mark with a stacked wordmark, set in near-black with one signal blue and quiet, generous type.

Palette
  • #0A0A0A
  • #00A8E8
  • #22C55E
  • #F59E0B
  • #F5F3EE
Type
Inter Tight for headlines and reading; Source Serif 4 as the accent face.
System
Numbered navigation, small uppercase section labels, and three pillars each keyed to its own colour.
