THE SIGNAL

A Novel Series

By Latarence Butts

The Story

In a world where consciousness can be digitized and transmitted across the cosmos, humanity discovers an ancient signal from a civilization that faced the same existential questions we grapple with today. As scientists race to decode the message, they uncover truths that challenge everything we believe about life, death, and what it means to be human.

"The signal arrived at 3:47 AM on a Tuesday, as most world-changing events do—without ceremony, without warning. It was not a message from the stars, but something far more unsettling: a message from ourselves, sent from a future that no longer existed."
— Chapter One
