Runwae Apparel

Started in Fayetteville, Arkansas. Its look comes from the label's first magazine: streetwear set into gilded Renaissance paintings, under the line "Everybody Influences."

Two lines are ready to come back. Fall '21 includes True Grit and Crafting. The Little Things is a one-color line of monograms, line-art faces and "Luv thy neighbor." Those faces also star in The Little Things, the studio's wordless animated shorts.
