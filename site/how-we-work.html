How we see design

Three habits behind every page, cover and garment.

Structure first

Every piece starts on a grid. The frame is drawn first, and everything else sits inside it.

One accent

Ink, bone and a single colour that carries the work. Each brand we make gets a colour of its own.

The working shows

Stages, versions and specs are set where you can read them, at the same care as the headline.

What starts a piece

Most of our work begins with something that already exists.

An old page

A sketchbook, a script, a line of clothing. Many of our titles were written or drawn years before we could finish them.

Someone else's story

Partner work starts from what the partner already has: their music, their gallery, their product.

A new tool

Modern production tools, AI among them, finish work that once needed a much larger team.

The stages of creation

Every title on the slate carries one of these.

Development

The story and the world are written or drawn. Drafts exist, and the shape is still changing.

Pre-production

The plan is set. Scripts, designs and the release format are prepared.

Production & release

The finished work is made, then released. Until then, working versions are sold with their stage named.
