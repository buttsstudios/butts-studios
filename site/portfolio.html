Latarence

Identity · Personal site

A one-page personal site built around a hand-drawn L² mark and a scribbled pen portrait. Light mode shows the portrait on paper; dark mode swaps in the mark, drawn in chalk on ink. The name is set in handwriting, with a quiet monospace footer.

Palette
  • #F5F3EE
  • #EDE8DF
  • #1A1916
  • #C8B87A
Type
Gloria Hallelujah for the name; Geist Mono for details; Spectral for reading.

Ridgefield

Identity · Website

An engineering firm for systems that have to perform in the field: hardware, software, simulation and AI. The identity is a solid square mark and a monospace voice, with ink, bone and one olive signal colour, faint terrain contours behind the type, and live demos you can steer in the browser.
