About

Kitch is a marketplace that connects brands with creators, with a focus on micro-influencers. Brands write a campaign brief, find creators by audience fit, and review profiles, past performance and rates.

Creators submit content for approval inside the platform, with feedback tools and version tracking. Payments are held in escrow and released by milestone once deliverables are approved and published.

Campaign analytics track impressions, engagement, clicks and conversions with exportable reports, and brands can draw an area on a map to target creators and audiences by region.
