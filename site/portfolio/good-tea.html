About

Good Tea, called Tea on its own site, gathers content from YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X into one feed. People connect accounts through read-only OAuth and follow creators across platforms in a single view.

AI analysis sorts content by topic, scores sentiment, flags emerging trends and links organic content to marketing campaigns. Saved items are tagged automatically and grouped into collections.

A browser extension captures content from any platform, libraries sync across devices, and data exports to CSV or JSON. The site names content strategists, brand managers and researchers as its main users.

Butts Studios designed the landing site.
