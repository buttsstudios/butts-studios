Based on a story to be true

The story

He's seventeen, a two-time first-team all-state guard, and awake on the operating table while a drill works through his shins. He counts ceiling tiles and won't look down. The Ivy League offers came weeks earlier, at a workout he could barely walk through.

From there the book follows one life forward: the recruiting, the research labs, Spain, a pilot's license, the road trips and the rooms where big decisions got made. It's written close to the bone and based on the author's own life. Somewhere along the way it asks how much of that life has happened yet, and how much is still being written.

What a feeling to be numb and feel at the same time. You can't imagine the pain you're escaping, but the little that you do is enough to keep you in fear. From the opening chapter, "Operation"

What it's about

Ambition and what it costs. Doing what it takes when you know it's wrong. Faith and isolation. Seeing the future before anyone around you can, and not always being right about it.

From the writer's notes

"Get lost in the story. Live, don't write. Go for the heart of the reader."
